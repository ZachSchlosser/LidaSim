What's in each profile

Option 1 — CastBench

Baseline characterization from the GPS-Bench / CastBench project. Each actor has 12 structured fields.

  • Identity (id, type, domain, country)
  • Grounded 2026 facts with citations
  • Leverage, dependencies, alignment
  • Numeric disposition: race_pull, internalization, openness
  • 5-axis harm weights (DISEMP, LOSSC, POWER, SOCID, MISUSE)

Option 2 additions

Two layers added on top of the Option 1 CastBench profile:

  • Behavioral Trend — ~100-word HumanLM-style paragraph tracing each actor's AI posture since 2012 using a 6-state lens (Belief/Goal/Emotion/Value/Stance/Communication).
  • Pairwise Relationships — Rivera-style matrix with short text descriptions (not numeric scores). 300 unique pairs, symmetric, displayed in both actors' sections.
