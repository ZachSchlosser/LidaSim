7. Existential / Frontier AI
7a Frontier AI — State Transparency California SB 53 — Transparency in Frontier AI Act CA S.B. 53 (2025) State-level safety duties for frontier developers above 10²⁶ FLOP; whistleblower protections; incident reporting; CalAISt; signed Sept 29, 2025 State Passed
7b Frontier AI — Federal Licensing Preserving American Dominance in AI Act (S. 5616) S. 5616 (118th Congress) Establishes AI Safety Review Office in Commerce; CFIUS-style pre-deployment reviews of frontier models for CBRN + cyber risks Federal Proposed
7c Frontier AI — Federal Comprehensive Governance Great American AI Act of 2026 (Obernolte-Trahan discussion draft) Discussion draft (June 4, 2026) Bipartisan 269-page federal framework: transparency duties for large frontier developers ($500M+), licensed Independent Verification Organizations, whistleblower protections, four titles (Governance, Workforce, Cyber, R&D); preempts state AI laws with Dec 2029 sunset Federal Proposed (discussion draft)
7d Frontier AI — Mandatory Third-Party Audits Illinois SB 315 — Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act IL S.B. 315 (signed July 6, 2026) Strongest state frontier-AI law: annual independent third-party safety audits of covered frontier developers, transparency reports, critical-safety incident reporting, whistleblower protections; administered by IEMA + AG State Passed
7e Frontier AI — State Transparency (NY) New York RAISE Act (S.6953B / A.6453B, Ch. 699) Ch. 699 — governor.ny.gov Requires large frontier AI developers to publish safety/security frameworks; mandatory 72-hour critical-harm incident reporting; creates oversight office within the NY Department of Financial Services; AG civil enforcement (up to $1M first violation, $3M subsequent); signed Dec 19, 2025 State Passed
7f Frontier AI — State Third-Party Auditing (MA) Massachusetts Frontier AI Transparency Act (S. 2630) MA S. 2630 (194th General Court) Applies to labs with >$500M AI-derived revenue or >$1B AI R&D spend; mandates frontier AI safety frameworks, independent third-party evaluators assessing catastrophic risks at least semi-annually, whistleblower protections, critical-incident reporting; AG injunctive enforcement; endorsed by Anthropic June 2026; carried as part of broader economic development bond bill State Proposed
7g Frontier AI — EU Risk-Based Framework EU AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — EUR-Lex Harmonized EU-wide risk-based framework: bans unacceptable-risk AI (social scoring, manipulative AI), imposes conformity-assessment + transparency obligations on high-risk AI (hiring, education, biometrics, critical infrastructure), GPAI model rules with systemic-risk tier; max fines 6% global annual turnover; phased application Aug 2024 – Aug 2027 EU Passed
7h Frontier AI — Federal Transparency & Independent Verification FRONTIER Act (Obernolte-Trahan) Section-by-Section (July 21, 2026) Federal frontier-AI governance bill. Defines "frontier model" (>10²⁶ FLOP) with tiered developer duties: large = $50M+ rev & $1B+ AI spend; very large = $5B+ rev & $10B+ spend. Mandates published frontier AI frameworks, annual independent compliance audits, pre-deployment transparency reports, and critical-safety incident reporting. Creates a new Under Secretary of Commerce for AI Security. Requires licensed Independent Verification Organizations (IVOs) for very large developers, with 72-hour imminent-catastrophic-risk referral to the Secretary. Authorizes emergency suspension/restriction orders (up to $10M/day civil penalty; willful violations criminal). Preempts closely related state frontier-safety statutes (CA SB-53, NY RAISE, IL SB-315). Bipartisan: Obernolte (R-CA) + Trahan (D-MA); section-by-section released July 21, 2026 Federal Proposed
