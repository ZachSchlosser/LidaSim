Actors in the AI 2027 & AI 2040 Scenarios

Unified inventory of every entity with agency across both scenario documents by the AI Futures Project. Compiled for the Simulating AI Policies agentic-testbed project. Each row is one unique actor, tagged with which scenario(s) it appears in. Use the chips and search to filter.
Sources: ai-2027.pdf (71pp) + AI-2040.pdf (90pp) · 174 unique actors · 105+94=199 document-actor links · 24 appear in both documents
